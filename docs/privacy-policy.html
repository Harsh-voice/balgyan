BalGyan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

BalGyan is a learning app for young children (ages 1.5–5) that teaches letters and numbers in Hindi, Marathi, and English. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles. The short version: we collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. BalGyan does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information — from children or adults. The app:

  • requires no account, sign-up, or login;
  • works fully offline and makes no network connections at runtime;
  • contains no advertising and no third-party advertising SDKs;
  • contains no analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally on your device only, so it can remember your preferences between sessions:

  • your chosen language (Hindi / Marathi / English);
  • the audio mute setting;
  • simple practice counts shown in the Parent Zone (e.g. "4 of 6 correct").

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Children's privacy

BalGyan is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families policy and applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA and GDPR-K) by the simplest possible means: it collects no data from anyone. Settings that adults use are placed behind a parental gate.

In-app purchases

The current version of BalGyan is completely free and contains no purchases. If paid learning modules are added in a future version, any purchase flow will sit behind a parental gate and this policy will be updated before release.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date revised before the change ships.
